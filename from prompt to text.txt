Artificial Intelligence, or AI, is becoming an important part of our daily life.
Even if we don’t notice it, we use AI almost every day.

For example, when you use your phone to unlock with your face, that is AI.
When you watch videos on YouTube or Netflix and get recommendations, that is also AI.
Even apps like maps that show the fastest route use AI to help you save time.

AI helps make our lives easier, faster, and more comfortable.
It can help students study better by giving smart suggestions and explanations.
It also helps doctors detect diseases and helps businesses improve their services.

However, it is important to use AI in a smart and responsible way.
We should not depend on it too much, but use it as a tool to help us learn and grow.

In conclusion, AI is not just technology—it is a powerful tool that is changing how we live, learn, and work every day.
